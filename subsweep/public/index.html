Questions

Straight answers.

Is my banking data safe?

Uploaded statements are analysed in memory for your session and never written to disk or shared. If you create an account, only the derived subscription list is saved — never your raw transactions. Bank connections use the regulated Consumer Data Right consent flow, so we never see your bank login.

Which banks work?

Statement upload works with CSV exports from all major Australian banks (CBA, Westpac, NAB, ANZ, ING, Up and more). Direct bank connection covers CDR-enabled institutions.

Do you cancel subscriptions for me?

We give you the fastest path — direct cancel links for 30+ services and a pre-written refund email for recent charges — but the final click is always yours, so you stay in control of your accounts.

How do refunds work?

Many services refund a charge if you ask within days of it landing. SubSweep flags charges inside that window and writes the request for you; you send it from your own email.

Is this financial advice?

No. SubSweep is a tool for reviewing your own spending on everyday services. It doesn't recommend financial products or provide financial advice.
