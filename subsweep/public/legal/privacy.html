2. What we do not do

  • We do not store raw transactions or statements. Uploads and bank data are processed in memory and discarded when your session ends.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your data to train models or for any purpose other than running SubSweep for you.
  • We do not provide financial product advice. SubSweep helps you review your own spending on subscriptions and does not recommend financial products.

3. Who we share data with

Only the service providers needed to run SubSweep, each limited to its role:

Basiq Pty Ltd (Australia) — accredited data recipient that retrieves your bank transactions under the Consumer Data Right when you connect a bank. Basiq's own privacy and CDR policies apply to its handling of your data.

Stripe — processes payments for SubSweep Pro. Card details are entered on Stripe's pages and never reach our servers.

Resend — sends account emails (verification, password reset, monthly monitoring).

Railway — hosts the application and database. Servers may be located outside Australia; we protect data in transit with TLS and at rest with access controls.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

4. Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes. Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies. Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate the service. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

5. How long we keep data

  • Raw transaction data: not kept. Discarded from memory at the end of your session or when you start over.
  • Analysis results and account details: for as long as your account exists.
  • Anonymous visitors: nothing is kept after the session ends.

6. Your rights

You can access or correct your account details in the app. To delete your account and all associated data, disconnect any bank first, then email {{EMAIL}} from your account address. We will confirm deletion within 10 business days. You can also turn off monthly monitoring at any time from the dashboard or the unsubscribe link in any monitoring email.

7. Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you logged in and to keep anonymous sessions separate. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

8. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, email {{EMAIL}} and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

9. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be emailed to account holders.
